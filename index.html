FarmKɔpɔ — digital payments & records for Sierra Leone's smallholder farmers

An offline-first platform for cooperatives, aggregators and agri-SMEs. Buyers record each produce purchase and pay the farmer straight to Orange Money, Afrimoney or QCell QMoney. Every transaction builds the farmer's digital record and a FarmScore that community banks, MFIs and FSAs can use, with the farmer's consent, to offer seasonal credit.

🌱 Farmer sells produce
⚖️ Agent records weight & grade (offline OK)
📲 Instant mobile-money payout + SMS receipt
💰 Auto-save a share for next season
📊 FarmScore → input loan

Agri DFS use cases this prototype covers

a
Digital payments: buyer-to-farmer payouts to mobile-money wallets

b
Savings: a "Save-as-you-sell" nudge locks part of each payment until planting season

d
Credit scoring: FarmScore built from transaction, savings and repayment data

f
Record keeping: sales and input-expense history for each farmer, also on USSD

g
Digital transformation: the cooperative's paper ledger becomes live data

How to use this demo (3 minutes)

  1. Open ① Buyer / Agent App. Switch Offline mode on, record a purchase, then switch it off. The queued payment syncs and the farmer gets an SMS receipt.
  2. Open ② Farmer USSD. Dial *384*55# on the feature phone to check payments, savings and FarmScore. There is a Krio menu option.
  3. Open ③ Cooperative Dashboard to see live totals, women's participation and volumes by crop.
  4. Open ④ Lender Portal. Pick a farmer who gave consent and send a loan offer. It then appears in the farmer's USSD menu.
All farmers, amounts and payouts here are simulated. Mobile-money and SaPS integration is planned for the pilot through MNO/aggregator APIs and the BSL regulatory sandbox.
